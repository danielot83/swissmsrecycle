Two ways to use the board

01 / 02
Offer

Give away what's spare

Unused reagents, standards close to expiry, spare parts, or an instrument nobody has time to fix — post it and let another lab put it to use instead of it sitting on a shelf.

Request

Ask before you buy new

Need a column, a calibration standard, or a part in a hurry? Post a request — someone in the network may already have it spare, saving cost and lead time.

Built for the network, not the public

Access is limited to people from Swiss MS platforms and laboratories. Log in with your account to browse and post — new here? Creating an account takes a minute.
