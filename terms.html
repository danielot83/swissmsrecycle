What this site is

SwissMsRecycle is a small, free, community-run board for the Swiss mass spectrometry community to offer or ask for spare lab equipment, consumables, and instrument parts. It isn't a company or a commercial service — it's run informally, on a best-effort basis.

No warranty, no liability

Listings are posted directly by users and are not checked or verified by SwissMsRecycle. We make no guarantee that any listing is accurate, safe, complete, or still available. Any exchange, shipment, or use of items found here is entirely between the people involved — SwissMsRecycle takes no responsibility for the condition of items exchanged, for any loss, damage, or dispute arising from an exchange, or for downtime, bugs, or data loss on the site itself. Use it at your own judgment, the way you'd treat any classifieds board.

What we store

When you create an account, we store your email, full name, and company/university/group — this information is shown next to the listings you post so other members know who they're dealing with. Listings you post (title, description, category, brand, model, condition, location, contact email, and an optional photo) are visible to any other logged-in member. Photos are resized to a small square in your browser before upload.

Any logged-in member can see any other member's name, organization, and email address — this is a closed community board, not a public one, but it isn't anonymous either.

Analytics

We use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics tool, to see roughly how many people visit the site and which pages they use. It doesn't use cookies, doesn't track you individually across sites, and doesn't collect your name or email — just aggregate page-view counts.

Deleting your data

You can delete your own listings and profile information at any time from Settings. Because this is a static site without a backend admin system, fully deleting your login (email + password) itself requires asking us directly — email daniel.ortiz@epfl.ch and we'll remove it.

Changes

This page may change as the site grows. If anything meaningful changes about how data is handled, we'll update the date at the top.
